Art 74

Art 74 is the Intro to Digital Media class I took at San José State University in Fall 2026. Every few weeks the class moved to a new digital form: glitch art, memes, video mashups, hypertext, and finally this website.

Each of these four pieces takes something familiar, like a famous painting, a kids' show, home video next to Cold War footage, or a Greek myth, and bends it into something new.
