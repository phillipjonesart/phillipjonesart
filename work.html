Work

My work is grouped by subject. Art 74 holds my class projects from San José State University. The rest are personal projects in drawing, painting, animation, and visual development.

Art 74 Glitch, meme, video mashup, and hypertext projects from my new media art class at SJSU. Space Astronauts, spacesuits, and the Moon, mostly from the early space race. Characters & History Historical figures, invented characters, and portrait and anatomy studies. Concept Art & Storyboards Vehicles, props, and storyboards: the pre-production side of animation. Landscapes & Places Backgrounds and buildings that set the mood before any character shows up. Other Personal Work Animation tests, redrawn masterpieces, and ideas about power and myth. Figure Drawing Timed graphite studies drawn from live models.
