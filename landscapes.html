Work

Landscapes & Places

In animation, the background sets the mood before any character shows up. These are my landscapes and buildings, made as personal practice in background painting and perspective.

Cliffside Manor

2026 · Digital painting, with pencil thumbnail

A night scene of a gated road leading up to a mansion on a sea cliff, under a crescent moon. It started as the loose pencil thumbnail shown second, where I worked out the layout. Then I painted it in a limited purple palette, like a background from an old animated film.

Cloud Field

2026 · Digital painting

Tall summer clouds over a windblown field, painted in flat shapes with simple lines in the grass. It's a study in keeping a background calm and simple so a character could stand in front of it.

Victorian House

2026 · Digital pen drawing

A Victorian house with a tall tower, bay windows, and flowering bushes out front. Houses like this are all over the Bay Area, and drawing all of the trim was practice in patience and perspective.
