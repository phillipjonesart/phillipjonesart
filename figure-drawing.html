Work

Figure Drawing

Figure Drawing Studies

2025 · Graphite on sketchbook paper

These are drawn from live models in timed poses, from five-minute gestures to forty-five-minute studies. The pose times are written in the corners.

Figure drawing is the base of everything else I make. It trains me to see proportion, weight, and how light moves across a body, which is what lets my characters feel believable.
