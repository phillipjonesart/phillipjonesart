Figure Drawing

2024 to 2025, graphite on sketchbook paper

These are drawn from live models in timed poses, from five-minute gestures to forty-five-minute studies. The pose times are written in the corners.

Figure drawing is the base of everything else I make. It trains me to see proportion, weight, and how light moves across a body.
