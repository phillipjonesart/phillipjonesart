About Me

I make traditional and digital drawings and paintings and short digital animations. I use traditional media like pencils, markers and paint, as well as Procreate and Photoshop. I am interested in concept art, character design, background paintings, prop design, storyboarding, and overall animation pre production and visual development. I am driven by telling stories and creating captivating visual imagery. I have not developed myself as an artist as much as I aspire to, and these interests are still aspirations I am working toward. I am still in a developmental stage of becoming an artist.I am interested in telling real stories from history as well as historical fiction, and I am influenced by art history and visual imagery from history. I am interested in all periods of history, particularly European history, American history, Indigenous stories, and science and natural history. I am drawn to stories that are lesser known. I am influenced by animation I watched growing up as well as current events and politics. I want to tell historical narratives that parallel modern political problems, as well as conditions of modern politics and geopolitics, particularly the role that capitalism and U.S. imperialism have played in the modern world.I want people to notice attention to detail in my work, accuracy to historical reference, and believable design and concepts. I prefer a more advanced and detailed style that bridges realism and 2D hand drawn animation.
